## Direction contract

THESIS: Devcom Digital's suite is a curated collection worth entering, not a feature-card grid — the redesign refuses the generic hero-plus-three-cards SaaS template the site currently ships.

OWN-WORLD: Charcoal ground (`#141110`-ish near-black, warm not blue-black), one committed brass/gold accent (`#c9973f`-ish) carrying 30-60% visual weight on Persuade surfaces, warm off-white type. Oversized confident display grotesk for headlines; tabular monospace numerals for price, counts, and the license key. Hairline gold rules mark section boundaries instead of boxed cards. Status (tool AVAILABLE/IN_DEVELOPMENT, license active/inactive, subscription active/inactive) renders as a literal lit-vs-dormant treatment — a glowing filled dot plus full-opacity gold text when live, a hollow ring plus dimmed text when dormant — never a soft rounded badge alone, threaded from the marketing page through dashboard and tools.

STORY: A marketing agency/freelancer lands, immediately reads "every tool, one credential" as the pitch, sees the tool suite presented like an award shelf (confident, plural, real), and understands subscribing issues one license key that lights up access across all of it. CTA is to view pricing or register; no invented proof/logos (none exist per PRODUCT.md).

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed charcoal hero. Left-aligned, generous top margin. Small gold kicker-less brand mark top-left (wordmark only, no eyebrow). Oversized headline (7xl+, tight tracking) stating the consolidation claim directly. One line of support copy at measured width. Two actions: filled gold primary, hairline-outline secondary. Below the fold line, a hairline rule, then a horizontal row of tool "entries" (award-shelf cards: name, one-line role, lit/dormant status dot) representing the real dynamic tool catalog, not fixed marketing copy.

FORM: Assigned direction (index 4 of 7 grounded candidates: agency award-show identity), seed key 6bf65291, raised by three donations — hairline/negative-space discipline (from the Brodovitch Bazaar-spread challenger), lit-vs-dormant state grammar (from the seven-segment-display challenger), tabular measured numerals (from the blacksmith-forge challenger).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
